Color palette: fresh green, sky blue, warm white, earthy brown, cheerful natural accents. Constraints: one coherent scene, no trophy, no winner, no sleeping rabbit, no lettering, no titles, no logos, no watermark, no embedded UI. This will be gently animated in the website, so make a clean finished illustration rather than a sprite sheet.
