About the platform

What is PABASA?

PABASA is a digital reading assessment and learning platform designed to help Grade 2 students develop reading skills while giving teachers tools to assess, guide, and monitor their progress.

How It Works

Assessment, practice, and teacher support connect to keep learning moving.

  1. 01

    Assess Reading

    Students participate in a teacher-guided reading assessment to identify their current reading level.

  2. 02

    Identify Learning Needs

    Results help teachers understand reading skills and areas for improvement.

  3. 03

    Practice and Learn

    Students complete prescribed and supplementary ARAL activities based on their needs.

  4. 04

    Monitor Progress

    Teachers review performance and subsequent assessments to guide continued support.
