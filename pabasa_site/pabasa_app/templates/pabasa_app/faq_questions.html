PABASA brings reading assessments and learning activities together for Grade 2 learners and their teachers. Teachers can review reading results and use them to plan support.

Explore how PABASA works ↗

Teachers and students have separate signup options. Choose the role you will use, then follow the instructions shown on the form. A parent or guardian can help a young learner complete student signup.

For an account already provided by your school, ask your teacher for access details before creating another account.

An assessment helps teachers understand a student’s current reading skills. Practice activities give students more opportunities to read, listen, and build those skills. Assessment results help teachers decide what support a learner may need next.

Prescribed ARAL activities follow the available lesson sequence. Supplementary activities are additional materials released and assigned for the learner’s class. Access depends on the learner’s current class and available work.
