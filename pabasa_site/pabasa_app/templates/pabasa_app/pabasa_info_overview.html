About the platform

What is PABASA?

Bringing reading assessment
and learning together.

PABASA, or the Program for Automated Basic Reading and Speech Assessment, brings reading assessments and learning activities together in one digital platform.

Built for Grade 2 learners and their teachers, it supports classroom reading work while keeping teachers involved in each child's learning.
