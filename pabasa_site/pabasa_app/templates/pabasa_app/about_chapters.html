Chapter 02 — Design Thinking

02 / 04

The Decisions
Behind the Design

Inside the PABASA design lab. Select a principle to explore the thinking.

Three principles.
One considered experience.

01 / The learner's next step

Make reading tasks easier to follow.

The challenge
Young learners need clear instructions and recognizable steps.
Our design response
PABASA uses guided narration, readable text, and straightforward activity interactions.
02 / The teacher's overview

Make assessment records easier to manage.

The challenge
Teachers need organized assessment information they can review without repeatedly consolidating separate records.
Our design response
PABASA centralizes assessment results, activity records, and monitoring tools.
03 / Encouragement through guidance

Make reading practice feel approachable.

The challenge
Reading activities should provide guidance without overwhelming young learners.
Our design response
PABASA provides guided instructions and supportive feedback while keeping reading tasks meaningful.
